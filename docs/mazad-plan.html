Egypt · online auctions · working name "Mazad" (مزاد)

Bid on anything. Pay only if you mean it.

Market findings, positioning, business plan and product build plan for an Egyptian auction marketplace with four screens (Explore, Buy, Sell, Profile) and a 1% bid deposit that proves buyers can pay. Written 16 Oct 2026.

Verdict: worth building, as a niche

A general "eBay for Egypt" is a weak bet. A focused auction app with a verified-buyer deposit and a clear trust story has a real shot.

Good sign

Payments are ready

InstaPay had about 12.5M users and nearly 1.5B transactions in 2024 (CBE). A 1% deposit hold is realistic through Paymob, Fawry or InstaPay.

Good sign

E-commerce keeps growing

One estimate puts Egypt at about $10.2B in 2025 and $19.3B by 2030. Estimates differ a lot by source, so read it as direction, not a fact.

Good sign

Your deposit idea fits the pain

Fake bidders and no-shows are the main reason auctions fail in cash-and-negotiation markets. A deposit solves it directly.

Warning

Several have tried

Belmazad, Mazadat, yaBalash, AllaOna and others exist. None looks dominant in general goods, and Dubizzle and Facebook Marketplace own second-hand.

Recommendation

Launch in one category where price is uncertain and fakes are a worry (sneakers, phones and electronics, or collectibles). Add verification for that category. Prove it with 20 to 30 real sellers and about 200 buyers before widening.

As a portfolio project it is strong regardless: realtime bidding, payments, concurrency and trust design.

Positioning ideas

Pick one. Each is a different promise to a different buyer.

Recommended

"Real buyers only"

Every bidder has put 1% down, so sellers never chase a no-show. Pair it with verified items in one category.

Tagline: مزاد بجد — a real auction.

The sneaker and streetwear vault

Authenticated pairs, drops that end on a countdown, a collector community.

Tagline: Every pair, checked. Every bid, real.

Smart used electronics

Phones and laptops with tested battery and IMEI check, starting at EGP 1 so the market sets the price.

Tagline: بالسعر اللي السوق يقوله — the market's price.

Collectors and vintage

Coins, watches, art, antiques. Value is subjective, which is where bidding wins most.

Tagline: Found something rare? Let people fight for it.

Voice: Egyptian Arabic first, light humor, no corporate tone. Franco-Arabic works in marketing. Keep prices in EGP, never show other currencies.

What could make it a hit

Auctions grow on excitement and trust. These are the levers, ranked by impact.

1. Trust you can see

Verified seller badge (national ID check), verified item tag, escrow-style payment release only after delivery is confirmed. This beats Facebook where scams are common.

2. Start at EGP 1, end with a story

"Open to highest bidder" listings create viral moments: someone sells a sofa for far more than expected and shares it. Feature these.

3. Live countdown feeling

Realtime price updates, "3 people watching", anti-sniping extension, outbid push notifications. This is what pulls people back daily.

4. Trending feed as the home screen

Your Explore tab: trending now, ending in the next hour, new today. It should feel alive even with few listings.

5. Delivery handled for them

Partner with Bosta, Aramex or similar for pickup and delivery, with cash on delivery for the final amount. Delivery friction kills most Egyptian marketplaces.

6. Seller tools that earn trust

Reserve price, scheduled start, relist in one tap, simple earnings page. Sellers are your scarce side, so make them feel looked after.

7. Share-first design

Every auction has a WhatsApp-ready card with a live countdown. Most Egyptian traffic starts in chats and groups.

8. Community and drops

Themed weekly auctions ("Friday phones", "Vinyl night"), creators hosting drops, leaderboards for top collectors.

Business plan

All figures below are planning assumptions to test, not facts.

Who pays, and how
  • Seller fee: 5% to 8% of the final price, only when it sells. Start at 5% to attract sellers.
  • Verification fee: flat EGP 50 to 150 per item for authentication or inspection (category dependent).
  • Boosts: featured listing on Explore, EGP 30 to 100.
  • Delivery margin: small mark-up on partner shipping.
  • Later: seller subscription for power sellers, ads from brands in the niche.

The 1% deposit is not revenue. It is held and refunded or applied. Payment partner holds the funds, which avoids holding customer money yourself.

Product: four screens

Mobile-first with a bottom tab bar. Desktop gets a top nav. Arabic and English with RTL, prices in EGP.

Tab 1

Explore

  • Trending now, ranked by bids and watchers in the last 24 hours
  • Ending soon with live countdowns
  • Category grid and chips
  • Realtime price on every card
Tab 2

Buy

  • Search plus filters: time left, budget, starting price, category, city, condition
  • Auction page with gallery, bid history, quick-bid buttons
  • First bid holds a 1% deposit, with a clear explanation
  • Watchlist and outbid alerts
Tab 3

Sell

  • Steps: photos, details, price mode, duration
  • Price mode: starting price, or open to highest bidder
  • Hidden reserve: "don't sell below X"
  • Timed or open-ended until the seller accepts
  • Seller dashboard with live bids
Tab 4

Profile

  • My bids: winning, outbid, won, lost
  • My listings and earnings
  • Wallet: top up, held amount, history
  • Verification status, language, settings

Auction rules

RuleDecision
Deposit1% of the starting price, held once per auction on a bidder's first bid. Losers are refunded automatically. The winner's deposit counts toward the price.
Open to highest bidderStarting price is EGP 1. No minimum. Use the reserve if the seller wants a floor.
Reserve priceHidden. If not met at close, no sale and everyone is refunded.
Minimum raiseIncrement scales with price (for example EGP 5 under 500, EGP 25 under 5,000, EGP 100 above).
Anti-snipingA bid in the last 2 minutes extends the end by 2 minutes.
Winner does not payDeposit is forfeited, strike on account, repeated strikes lead to a ban. The seller can offer the item to the next bidder.
Seller conductSellers cannot bid on their own items. Cancelling after bids needs a reason and affects seller rating.

The deposit rule needs a legal check, since holding customer funds can require a licensed payment partner.

Build plan

Already approved: responsive web on Next.js, Supabase backend, mock wallet first, Paymob later.

Stack
  • Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn/ui
  • Supabase: Postgres, Auth, Storage, Realtime
  • Bidding and closing live in Postgres functions place_bid and close_auction, so the rules cannot be bypassed from the browser
  • A scheduled job closes ended auctions
  • Payments behind one adapter: mock wallet now, Paymob or Fawry later
Data model
  • profiles with wallet balance and held amount
  • categories, auctions, bids
  • deposits (held, refunded, applied)
  • wallet_tx ledger
  • watchlist

Risks and how to handle them

RiskImpactResponse
Not enough sellers or buyers at onceHighOne niche, one city, hand-recruited sellers, themed auction nights to concentrate bidders.
Scams and wrong itemsHighID-verified sellers, verified-item tag, payment released after delivery confirmation.
Sellers take winners off-platformMediumLow fees, escrow protection, ratings and seller tools that are only useful on-platform.
Free competitors with huge audiencesMediumDo not compete on listings volume. Win on trust and excitement in one category.
Buyers dislike the depositMediumExplain it as a refundable guarantee, offer referral credit, measure conversion and tune.
Payment and legal rulesHighUse a licensed payment partner to hold funds. Get a local lawyer to review terms and consumer rules before launch.
Delivery qualityMediumStart with local pickup and one courier partner. Track delivery success as a core metric.
